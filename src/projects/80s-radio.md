only80sRadio is a minimal internet radio designed to solve the problem of radios playing music other than 80s music. Modern radios come with a tuner, which means you have to manually tune a dial yourself until you finally find the 80s channel, wasting valuable time and fine motor skills that are better spent elsewhere. Ain't nobody got time for that. Built around an ESP32-S2 Mini microcontroller, only80sRadio is designed to always be pre-tuned to the 80s station. Just flip the ON switch and the radio knows EXACTLY what you came for. (Can also be configured to play other types of internet radio stations if that becomes necessary.) You'll need an ESP32-S2 Mini board, a MAX98357A I2S amplifier, a 3W 4Ω speaker. Add in a rechargeable 3.7V Li-ion battery with a compatible charging/boost module and on/off switch to make it portable. Get creative with the enclosure. We chewed a bunch of gum, 1980s style, and then fit our prototype into the empty gum container (see photos). Try and beat that.

For details, see the [github repository](https://github.com/tranquility-base-0/only80sRadio).
